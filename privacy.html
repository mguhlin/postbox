Your email stays on your device.

Postbox, created by Miguel Guhlin, opens exported email locally. The Go desktop app and portable browser edition do not intentionally send imported email, attachments, searches, or exports to an outside service. They have no account, analytics, advertising, AI API, or cloud-save feature. External developer/support links are optional online destinations.

What Postbox handles

Message headers, addresses, dates, subjects, bodies, attachments, archive names, and Outlook folder names are processed in memory. Those records can contain personal, school, health, employment, or other sensitive information. Postbox does not anonymize them.

CopyLocation and clearing
Imported records and search textMemory in the local mail service and application window. Clear session removes records, plans, reader text, previews, selections, and search inputs. Closing the app/service ends its session.
Temporary parsing filesMBOX/PST parsing uses a local temporary directory and removes it after normal parsing. A crash or forced termination can leave temporary artifacts.
Original archivesRemain on disk. Session-only deletion preserves them. Original-file edits require a separate mode choice, confirmation and content checks.
Saved files and printed outputExports, attachments, PDFs, print spools, and printouts remain in your chosen destinations. Clear session does not delete them.
Device and system copiesOS swap, crash reports, filesystem snapshots, browser/webview metadata, backups and security software are controlled by your device and organization. Clear session is not a secure disk-erasure operation.

Connections and device access

The mail engine listens on the loopback interface, not a public network address. The browser edition checks its hostname/origin and requires a random session token for changes; it is intended for a trusted single-user device and does not isolate different local OS users or programs. The desktop edition additionally keeps its mail service behind a private per-launch key. File access is granted through user-selected files/folders, and native file operations are restricted to managed sources or chosen export destinations.

Message HTML is sanitized and isolated. Scripts, remote images, navigation, and network-loading CSS are removed or blocked. PDF previews use bundled local PDF.js and do not run PDF scripts or embedded actions. Saving a file and opening it in another application is a separate choice; that application has its own behavior and permissions.

The public download website is hosted by GitHub Pages; visiting it or downloading a release sends normal request metadata to GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. This is separate from local mail processing. See GitHub's privacy statement. Postbox does not control GitHub's logs or your OS/browser's own services.

Before using school records

Postbox is a general-purpose archive viewer, primarily for adults managing their own or authorized archives. It is not a student messaging service. Use only records you are authorized to access, on an approved device, with a suitable backup and retention plan. Your institution decides whether local processing, exported copies, disability access, and retention meet its obligations. No school authorization, executed DPA, FERPA/COPPA compliance, or accessibility certification is claimed.

The U.S. Department of Education recommends checking school/district approval before using an application with education records; its guidance explains the school-official conditions. Any use involving children, sensitive records, or another jurisdiction needs the responsible institution's assessment.

Accessibility and feedback

The interface aims for WCAG 2.2 AA, with native controls, keyboard access, visible focus and responsive layout. Automated scans and sampled workflows do not establish full conformance. Imported messages and files may themselves be inaccessible; extracted PDF text and DOC/DOCX previews have layout and reading-order limits. Physical screen-reader and OS print-dialog validation remain outside automated coverage. Report an issue using synthetic examples, never private email or student records. The repository's VCC review records the checks and their limits.
